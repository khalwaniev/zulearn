Problem practice ↗

Unseen challenge examinations

Research-adjusted proof and algorithm challenges. Difficulty is not asserted equivalent to official contest standards.

Putnam-style

Two three-hour proof exams requiring novel combinations of probability, linear algebra and optimization.

IMO-style

Deep discrete and mathematical reasoning with complete, independently checked proofs.

Schweitzer-style

Extended, open-ended mathematical investigation with full proof, limitations and source accounting.

IOI / ICPC-style

Correct and efficient graph, sequence and scientific algorithms under time, memory and hidden test constraints.
