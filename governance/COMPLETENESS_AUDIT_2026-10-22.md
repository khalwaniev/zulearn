# Zulearn scientific completeness audit — 2026-10-22

**Decision: FOUNDATION ACCEPTED; CURRICULUM NOT YET COMPLETE.**

This is an evidence-limited audit of the publicly committed Zulearn 2.0 repository. It evaluates implemented material, not aspiration, personal capability, or the merits of the learner.

## Observed state

- 32 tracked files at initial root commit `6a3c07c`.
- Approximately 7,200 Markdown words in the distributed ZIP that matches the checked live repository samples.
- `curriculum/TRIPOS.md`: four parts, 18 named modules; lacks full per-module syllabuses, exercise sets, worked marking schemes, and recorded examinations.
- `assessment/ELITE_EXAMS.md`: five original sample prompts; not a psychometrically calibrated IMO/Putnam/Miklós/IOI/ICPC equivalent.
- `practice/DEEP_ML.md`: prioritization and recording policy; no catalog-level problem IDs or completion records.
- `reading/PAPERS.md`: seed of several dozen papers and search candidates; no exhaustive affiliation-verified SFB/IVUL bibliographies, full reading notes, or independent reproductions.
- `reading/BOOKS.md`: 17 starting books, no selected chapter-to-skill-to-exam registry.
- `reading/COURSES.md`: 10 core table rows, no detailed assignment/examination mappings.
- `reading/REPOSITORIES.md`: 15 source-code targets, no component-level study plan and independent implementation record.
- `research/AGENTIC_ENGINEERING.md`: policy and target skills, no executable agent evaluations or threat model.
- `research/COMPETITIONS.md`: goals and ethics, no CASP/CAFA training benchmark matrix.
- Public GitHub is operational, but public learning and research outputs cannot yet substantiate earned mastery.

## Scope contract

**Must develop deeply:** proof-based mathematics, statistics and scientific inference, numerical optimization and scientific computing, foundational ML, representation learning, transformers, GNNs, geometry/equivariance, scientific CV and tracking, molecular/cellular biology, bioinformatics/cheminformatics, scientific interpretation, computational research engineering, and AI-agent reliability.

**Practice:** near-exhaustive Deep-ML relevance-filtered catalogue; well-selected Rosalind/LeetCode; occasional calibrated official algorithms and mathematics archives.

**Deferred/excluded by default:** finance-specific ML, RL-specialist tracks, consumer recommender products, algorithmic rating grind, unrelated analytics. Generic transferable methods are not permanently banned.

**Public/private boundary:** this repository must contain only public STEM materials and safely shareable professional progress. Personal objectives belong ONLY in a separate private repository.

## Standards

1. Curriculum choices must be **evidence-based but not misrepresented as experimentally optimized for this individual**.
2. All resources must have primary links and checked publication/last-review dates where important.
3. Learning completion never equals mastery. Track `planned`, `studied`, `independently passed`, `externally reviewed`, `reproduced`, `published` separately.
4. No claim of equivalence with elite universities, awards, labs, or researchers without independent comparable assessment. Inspiration is not accreditation.
5. Scholarly investigations should report exact retrieval methodology, scope, exclusions, author ambiguity, incomplete access, and source provenance.
6. No exhaustive reading of all online content. Curate by marginal research relevance, prerequisites, original methodological content, transfer, and feasibility.
7. All future curriculum expansion must create **executable learning work**: problems, proofs, software, examinations, scientific tasks, or review artifacts—not just more names.
8. Protect sleep, training sustainability, research ethics, and human benefit.
