# IELTS Academic: Band 9 as an aspirational target

Band 9 denotes expert language control; scoring depends on four components. Official: https://www.ielts.org/take-a-test/your-results/ielts-scoring-in-detail and https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/teach-ielts/test-information/assessment

## Plan
- Week 1: one official timed full mock, calibrated feedback on speaking and writing.
- Listening/reading: error taxonomy, question-type drills, timed sections, unfamiliar accents and scientific reading.
- Writing: 2 essays + 1 Task 1 response weekly, reviewed against official band descriptors; rewrite after feedback.
- Speaking: weekly recorded mock, pronunciation/fluency/precision feedback from a qualified evaluator.
- Research English: each week write a concise paper critique and orally defend one result.
- Retest monthly; register for the official test when official practice scores reliably meet the desired performance.

## Priority rule
Secure the minimum English requirement for actual PhD intake first. Then pursue Band 9 if marginal preparation has lower cost than critical research obligations. A 9 is never a prerequisite to scientific excellence.
