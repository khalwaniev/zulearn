## Application artifacts
- Accurate transcript and degree eligibility evidence.
- English language test according to the actual requirements applicable at submission.
- Strong research CV and honest public technical portfolio.
- Two/three rigorous project case studies, with clear personal contributions and results.
- Specific research statement and three qualified referees.
- 10-minute research talk + derivation defense + deep questions on validation and project limitations.
