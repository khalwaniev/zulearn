# Short Anki retrieval

One card tests one primary idea. Use definitions, conditions, formulas, short explanations, intuitions and recurring misconceptions. Keep long derivations, code projects and proofs as independent practice, not enormous cards. Do not turn every sentence into a card.

For today, these two original Basic cards are sufficient:

| Front | Back |
|---|---|
| What does a fraction denominator count? | The equal-sized parts that make one whole. |
| Why can 1/2 and 2/4 name the same amount? | Splitting each half into two equal parts gives two quarters without changing the amount. |

After learning, ask ChatGPT for at most a few similarly short drafts, check their correctness yourself, and add them to Anki. Review due cards using Anki's existing scheduler; do not build or maintain a second scheduling engine. Cards support memory, not a claim of proof, implementation or transfer mastery.

For bulk import, request UTF-8 tab-separated Front, Back, Tags, one note per row; inspect the preview and matching-front/update-existing behavior to avoid duplicates. Follow the [official Anki text-import manual](https://docs.ankiweb.net/manual/importing/text-files). Manual entry of today's two cards is simpler than an export pipeline.
