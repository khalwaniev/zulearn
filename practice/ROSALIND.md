# ROSALIND — bioinformatics olympiad track

Official: https://rosalind.info/problems/list-view/

Prioritize:
1. Stronghold: sequence processing, probability, alignment, motifs, graph problems.
2. Bioinformatics Textbook Track: alignment DP, peptide spectrum graphs, HMM, Viterbi, Baum–Welch, evolutionary models.
3. Algorithmic Heights: graphs, trees and combinatorics relevant to genomic analysis.
4. Armory: appropriate biological tools and databases.

**Rules:** attempt hidden tests without assistance; write biological interpretation, complexity, numeric correctness, and follow-up critique. Skip trivial items after diagnostic demonstration; solve significant textbook-track problems thoroughly.

Use it alongside Deep-ML, not as a replacement.
