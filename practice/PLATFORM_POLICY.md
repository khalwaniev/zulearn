## Status and evidence

Use **required, completed, complementary, duplicated, deferred, excluded, unresolved**. Record a reason for deferral/exclusion and identity evidence for duplication. Trading/betting contexts remain excluded; general mathematical skills can be learned through appropriate tasks. No global exemption is inferred from a degree, paper, leaderboard score or AI-assisted success.

Completion is an activity record. Independent competence needs its own unaided work, explanation/proof, actual execution where appropriate, later retrieval and transfer evidence. The [inventory](../data/catalogue/README.md) preserves all available factual observations; missing inner catalogue data stays unresolved. Item-level curation progresses during learning rather than delaying it.
