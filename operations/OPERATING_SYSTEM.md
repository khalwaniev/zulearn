# Zulearn operating system — anti-overload constraints

## Single source of truth
Choose **1 active flagship research project**, **1 primary mathematics course**, **1 secondary scientific discipline**, and **1 compact coding problem queue** per 2-week sprint. Everything else remains backlogged.

## Workload
For a full-time independent learning period: trial 35–45 hours of focused work per week and adjust for commitments and actual progress. Do not count unattended GPU runtime as study. Preserve sleep, exercise and recovery.

## Default 40-hour split (revise after diagnostics)
- 10 h mathematical problem sheets and derivations
- 7 h algorithms / Deep-ML / Rosalind
- 12 h research experiments, reproductions, engineering
- 7 h biology/chemistry and relevant primary paper reading
- 4 h revision, spaced retrieval, research writing, review

## Sprint gates
Daily: retrieval + minimum 1 unseen problem or experiment analysis.
Weekly: 1 closed-book evaluation; corrected error log; decide what to defer.
Every 4 weeks: oral defense, short report, updated proof/implementation portfolio.
Every 12 weeks: written cumulative examinations and rigorous independent critique.

## Evidence/status vocabulary
planned / started / studied / solved / independently verified / reproduced / externally reviewed / published. Never equate them.

## Research ethics and personal privacy
STEM only in this repository. Personal goals, identifying documents, health information, private religious study goals, personal life, and private application drafts must NEVER enter public git history. No API keys or unlicensed datasets. Keep separate private repository with correctly configured visibility before first commit; retroactive deletion cannot reliably erase public history.

## Decision-making
If a topic cannot be connected to an active scientific need or foundational transfer, archive it as `DEFERRED` for the next quarter. Do not create infinite obligations.
