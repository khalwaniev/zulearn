# AVOID.md — scope control, not permanent scientific prohibitions

## EXCLUDE as planned specializations
- AI for finance (trading signals, alpha discovery, credit-scoring business applications).
- Consumer/advertising recommenders and social-media ranking systems.
- Business marketing analytics and unrelated commercial ML niches.
- Unrelated competitive-programming platforms unless justified by a concrete research skill gap.

## DEFER unless research requires it
- Reinforcement learning, policy gradients, game-playing, robotics/control, and RLHF as research specialties.
- Full-stack web development, mobile apps, frontend design, blockchain, DevOps certifications.
- Generic data analytics beyond skills required to diagnose real scientific datasets.
- Niche ML algorithms without direct mathematical or research transfer.
- Competitive programming 'rating grinding' and Olympiad-style mathematics unrelated to research.

## DO NOT discard transferable foundations
- Matrix factorization and graph ranking (biological networks and drug-target interactions).
- Bandits / active learning / Bayesian optimization (scientific experimental design).
- Sequential decision-making (automation and adaptive experiments) when needed.
- Reinforcement-learning-adjacent model-based optimization if a future protein-design result genuinely requires it.
- Information retrieval and embeddings (scientific search and research agents).

## Exception rule
A deferred topic re-enters only when an active project demonstrates (1) a concrete blocker, (2) direct mechanism-to-task relevance, (3) no cheaper alternative, and (4) an explicit time budget and completion criterion.

## Weekly anti-scope-creep audit
Record which topics consumed time without advancing foundations, flagship research, reproducibility, or the application portfolio. Drop such topics for the next week.
