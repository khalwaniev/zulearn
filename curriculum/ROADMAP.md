# Adaptive Zulearn roadmap

**Start:** 2026-10-22. **Application intent:** around September 2027; date and entry cycle are user targets, not confirmed university deadlines.

## Competence tree (prerequisites before breadth)

### A. Mathematics
1. Linear algebra: spaces, orthogonality, spectral methods, SVD, matrix calculus.
2. Calculus and analysis: multivariable derivatives, Taylor expansions, gradients, constrained optimization.
3. Probability: random variables, conditional expectation, concentration, stochastic processes.
4. Statistics: estimation, hypothesis testing, dependent samples, uncertainty, multiple comparisons.
5. Optimization: convexity, duality, stochastic optimization, first/second-order methods.
6. Numerical analysis: conditioning, stability, linear solvers, floating-point and automatic differentiation.
7. Geometry: symmetries, SE(3), rotations, equivariance, differential geometry when required.
8. Scientific mathematics: dynamical systems, thermodynamics, information theory, inverse problems.

### B. Computational AI
1. Statistical ML, kernels, probabilistic graphical models, factorization.
2. Deep learning optimization; CNN, U-Net, ResNet, Transformers, GNNs.
3. Self-supervised, generative, multimodal, geometric and efficient foundation models.
4. Efficient scientific systems: data quality, profiling, GPU kernels, quantization, distributed training where relevant.

### C. Scientific understanding
1. Biology: central dogma, evolution, proteins, cells, genomics, omics.
2. Chemistry: bonding, stereochemistry, organic chemistry, physical/analytical chemistry.
3. Biophysics: structural methods, free energy, conformations, molecular interactions.
4. Data-generating process and experimental limitations.

### D. Vision
2D classification/segmentation → instance tracking → 3D medical imaging → geometric CV → video/temporal learning → multimodal scientific models → uncertainty/domain shift.

### E. Research independence
Find problem → literature map → falsifiable hypotheses → baseline and leakage audit → controlled experiments → uncertainty/ablations → reproduction package → manuscript and external feedback.

## Phase 1: diagnostics and 12-week foundation sprint
- Closed-book diagnostic in math, ML, systems, biology, vision, scientific validation.
- Pick 2 foundational gaps, 1 research project, 1 scientific domain to deepen.
- Build minimal correct implementations and timed unfamiliar problem solutions.
- Publish demonstrable derivations, tests, and research notes.

## Phase 2: following 6 months
- Deep-ML relevant catalogue by tags and adaptive difficulty.
- Two rigorous paper reproductions (one CV, one molecular/biomedical).
- Intensive scientific curriculum and reproducible project reports.
- IELTS baseline then targeted skill improvement.

## Phase 3: to KAUST application (~September 2027 target)
- One flagship research contribution, with honest novelty assessment and evidence.
- Strong letters, English qualifications, research CV, statement tailored to lab, interview defense.
- Referee deadlines and actual admissions cycle must be verified on the official site.

## Phase 4: multi-year research formation
Independent research question selection, collaborations with experimental scientists, methods innovation, scientific validation and follow-up studies. Competitive medals are optional outcomes, not prerequisites to scientific impact.

## Weekly allocation (illustrative; adjust for workload)
- 10h mathematics and theory
- 7h independent coding and Deep-ML
- 8h primary scientific research
- 5h biology/chemistry
- 4h literature and writing
- 3h research engineering / agents
- 3h English and scientific communication
**Total 40h of focused work.** Reduce to 20–30h when required; do not compromise sleep or scientific rigor.

## Mastery condition
A concept is passed only after an independently solved unseen task, correct explanation of assumptions and failure modes, working implementation where appropriate, and delayed retest.
