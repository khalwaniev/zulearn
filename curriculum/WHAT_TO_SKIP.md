# Skip, defer and preserve

**Skip/exclude:** finance ML specialization; ad-tech and TikTok-style recommender systems; certificates without skill proof; generic CP rating races; platform-hopping; consuming every YouTube video; indiscriminate paper bookmarking.

**Defer:** deep RL/RLHF; robotics; advanced game theory; commodity MLOps certification tracks; unrelated web-app frameworks; olympiad geometry/number theory without clear mathematical transfer.

**Retain transferable foundations:** dynamic programming, graph theory, matrix factorization, stochastic optimization, experiment design, bandits / Bayesian optimization for scientific exploration, information theory, geometry, rigorous proof.

**Exception rule:** re-enter a deferred area only with a concrete model/research bottleneck, direct potential benefit, measurable experiment and capped time budget.

See [`AVOID.md`](AVOID.md) for formal boundaries.
