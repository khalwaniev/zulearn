# Scientific evidence register and uncertainty report

> Version 1, 2026-11-27: bounded corrective revision after human-reported conditional independent approval. Curriculum architecture fixed for learning; local queues and learner evidence remain adaptive. Live GitHub integration awaits approval of this revised package.

This register separates recovered findings from newly checked facts and newly authored operating choices. It is a recovery synthesis, not a repeated systematic review. Date of targeted verification: **2026-11-27**. Primary sources and official course/platform pages are used; inaccessible full texts are not represented as read.

**Evidence classes:** `EMPIRICAL` findings from the cited study/review in its studied setting; `FACT` observed source/catalogue/institutional information; `INFERENCE` reasoned application to Zulearn; `JUDGMENT` local operational design; `UNRESOLVED` insufficient evidence. Evidence supporting a learning mechanism does not prove this particular resource sequence optimal.

**Verification flags:** `V` page/full text or relevant content newly inspected; `A` primary abstract/metadata newly inspected, not a full methods audit; `R` citation/conclusion retained from recovered report/repository, with new access limited or not repeated; `B` relevant access blocked. Multiple flags describe different access levels, not a numerical quality ranking.
