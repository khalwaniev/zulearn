# Research-aligned competition policy

> Version 1, 2026-11-27: bounded corrective revision after human-reported conditional independent approval. Curriculum architecture fixed for learning; local queues and learner evidence remain adaptive. Live GitHub integration awaits approval of this revised package.

Competitions are bounded research/assessment opportunities. The long-term targets ARC, CASP and CAFA do not create three simultaneous obligations. Keep one primary competitive research target, preserve already authorized commitments, and open a new competition only when the foundational opportunity cost is justified. The curriculum is not optimized for badges, ratings or submission counts.

Read actual rules for external data, training cutoffs, inference internet, code/data licenses, compute, submissions, teaming and publication before entry. Do not import last year's rule or a public notebook's practice as authority. Prize eligibility and benchmark purity require concrete evidence, not assumptions.

## During and after

Maintain a frozen baseline, experiment registry, budget and protected test boundary. Treat public-leaderboard feedback as adaptive exploratory information; it is not independent scientific validation. A model or benchmark exploit may improve a score without advancing the intended competence. Quantify scientific/engineering claims using proper controls and compute accounting.

After a bounded contest: classify one error, repair its prerequisite, independently reconstruct a solution, and test a new related task later. For a substantial research competition: retain method/data/code provenance, negative results, generalization/failure analysis and a concise report. A medal is valuable evidence of that competition result, not proof of mastery of all five research domains.

Pause or decline a new event when it displaces foundational repair, an existing flagship deliverable, adequate recovery or a more relevant experiment. This is a prioritization rule, not abandonment of long-term AI-for-Science objectives. Do not use unannounced future competitions to justify unrelated curriculum expansion.
