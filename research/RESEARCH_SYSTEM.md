# Research operating standards

## Minimum scientific research artifact
1. Scientific question and hypothesis; impact if solved.
2. Literature map: prior work, contradictions, unanswered gap.
3. Data provenance, data-generating process, approvals, license.
4. Strict leakage and distribution-shift audit.
5. Baselines, validation freeze, experiment registry with seeds/configs/hashes.
6. Statistical uncertainty, clinically/scientifically meaningful effect sizes, ablations, negative controls.
7. Failure analysis and alternative explanations.
8. Reproduction steps and costs; no hidden manual interventions.
9. Research report with limitations, negative results and independent review.

## Paper reading tiers
- T1: abstract/claims/exact experimental setting.
- T2: mathematics, architecture, datasets, metrics, supplementary methods.
- T3: small correct independent implementation, unit tests, independent replication.
- T4: falsifiable critique, controlled extension, original experiment.

## Output quality
Avoid unverified novelty, inflated SOTA claims, unmeasured computational efficiency, cherry-picked seeds, public-test contamination, opaque agent modifications, selective presentation of favorable results.

## Scientific value
Prefer a meaningful, trustworthy improvement on an important problem over a brittle score increase. Prioritize reproducibility, collaborations with experimental scientists and accessible beneficial applications.
