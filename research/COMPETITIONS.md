# Competition strategy: scientific method above scoreboard

## Kaggle top-1 aspiration
Select only competitions aligned to scientific modeling. 1–2 primary competitions at a time. Prioritize host rules, leakage-resistant validation, reliable reproducible baselines, disciplined experiments, resource accounting and trustworthy ensembles. Public leaderboard may be misleading; final private performance cannot be guaranteed. Record baselines and negative results.

## CASP
Official CASP17 (2026) modeling season ended in August 2026. Review CASP17 targets and forthcoming evaluation after announcement; then prepare for the next officially announced CASP edition. Never label an unannounced future CASP schedule as confirmed.
Source: https://predictioncenter.org/casp17/
Track research: proteins, complexes, RNA, ligand binding, ensembles, template/sequence leakage, robust physical validation, geometry and model confidence.

## CAFA
Critical Assessment of Functional Annotation. Follow the official organizers and currently open round; maintain a benchmark harness on ontology-aware labels, function prediction, temporal splits, sequence redundancy, Fmax/AUPR/other official metrics as applicable to each round. Verify schedule, track rules and metrics against official competition page rather than relying on past editions.

## Guardrails
Aiming to win CASP, CAFA and Kaggle simultaneously is an aspiration, not a plan with guaranteed outcomes. Use explicit annual priority and depth; independent biological evidence carries value even without a medal.
