# Elite laboratory capabilities map

These laboratories inspire problem choice and research standards; studying their output is not equivalent to supervised membership, experimental infrastructure, or a degree.

| Lab | Zulearn capability |
|---|---|
| Xin Gao SFB (KAUST) — https://sfb.kaust.edu.sa/ | sequence→structure→function; graph algorithms; probabilistic models; multimodal scientific ML; drug and healthcare AI |
| Bernard Ghanem IVUL (KAUST) — https://ivul.kaust.edu.sa/ | computer vision; video; multimodal representations; 3D visual models |
| Marinka Zitnik Lab (Harvard) — https://zitniklab.hms.harvard.edu/ | molecular and biological network AI, therapeutics |
| David Baker Institute for Protein Design (UW) — https://www.ipd.uw.edu/ | protein design, generative methods, experimental feedback |
| Regina Barzilay group (MIT) — https://www.regina.csail.mit.edu/ | molecular and clinical AI |
| David E. Shaw Research (molecular dynamics) — https://www.deshawresearch.com/ | molecular simulation and physical modeling; distinct from academic 'Dror Lab' |

**Avoid ambiguity:** 'Dror Lab' generally refers to the Ron Dror research group, https://drorlab.stanford.edu/. The separate D. E. Shaw Research group also advances molecular dynamics. Keep provenance and group identities distinct.

Zulearn cannot promise equal competence to any graduating scientist from these organizations. It can set specific, inspectable standards in mathematics, software, scientific knowledge, and original research.
