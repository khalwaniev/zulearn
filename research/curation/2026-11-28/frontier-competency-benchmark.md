## Evidence levels for the learner

Use the retained mastery protocol: assisted practice, independent performance on this instance, later retention, fresh transfer and externally reviewed research are separate records. A good answer immediately after reading a solution is assisted learning. A familiar archive item cannot certify unseen transfer. The 14 protected challenge definitions, original graders, allowed tools and exposure rules remain unchanged.

| Dimension | Observable artifact and success criterion | Failure that requires repair |
|---|---|---|
| Elementary quantitative meaning | Draw equal parts with a fixed whole; translate visual, fraction, decimal and number-line forms; justify each operation and nonzero divisor | Correct-looking manipulation without a valid unit/reference whole; confusion between ratio and fraction of total |
| Proof construction | Give assumptions, quantified claim, valid base/induction or other argument, and answer an objection on a fresh task | Numerical examples substituted for proof, a missing initial transition, or a reversed implication |
| Algorithms and TCS | Own implementation, invariant/termination proof, computational model, time/space analysis and adversarial cases checked against a small oracle | Passing visible cases while violating a boundary, confusing expected/amortized/worst-case work, or claiming timeout proves impossibility |
| Numerical mathematics | Derive dimensions and gradients; check exact cases, stability/conditioning and a range of finite-difference step sizes | Small residual treated as small solution error; overflow ignored; dtype/backend differences hidden |
| ML architecture | Derive one mechanism, implement it without copying, verify analytic/reference cases, and compare a simpler baseline under a declared budget | Guided block implementation described as paper-scale reproduction; training loss used as the sole validity criterion |
| Experimental inference | Define estimand and independent units, freeze split/metric/tuning scope, use paired controls and valid uncertainty, retain negative outcomes | Test-driven selection, image-level resampling of clustered specimens without justification, unmatched compute or unexecuted numbers |
| Scientific communication | Defend the main equation and causal claim; distinguish implementation, reproduction and extension; revise after substantive criticism | Fluent narrative unsupported by the actual artifact or inability to explain assumptions |
| Domain validity | Trace labels, measurement, accession and annotation dates; preserve charge/stereochemistry/ontology semantics; obtain qualified domain review where needed | Generic graph performance treated as chemical or biological validation; unknown annotations treated as verified negatives |

A local progression rule is two varied unaided answers with sound explanations, followed later by a changed retention sample. It is a workload heuristic, not a validated threshold. Increase practice when errors or failed transfer identify a specific missing operation. Do not require every problem in the pool before proceeding.

## Five frontier research contracts

| Objective and registry | Bounded study and measurable independent criterion | Essential adversarial comparison |
|---|---|---|
| AGI and world models: R01, L18–L22 | A finite family with an explicit observation/action model; report acquisition/generalization versus interactions and compute on held-out families. Keep prediction error, multi-step rollout and actual task utility separate | True-model planner, learned-model planner and a simple baseline; matched interactions/compute; partial-observability and model-error controls |
| RSI and meta-learning: R02, L22, M23, C09/C10/C17 | Specify what changes itself, what feedback is available, and what counts as improvement; freeze an evaluator and report total effort including proposal/search/evaluation and human interventions | Repeated tuning without self-change, reuse-only and proposed mechanism; held-out task families and no evaluator selection leakage; theorem conditions distinguished from practical behavior |
| Reasoning and invention: R03, L28, C04/C08/C10 | Construct a typed finite search space and checked oracle; report correct solutions, false acceptance, coverage, failures and cost on held-out families | Enumeration/simple search, learned proposal and verifier variants at equal candidate/token/compute budgets; faulty-verifier controls; final-answer and step validity separated |
| Mechanistic interpretability: R04, L24, M24 | Pick one behavior and operational mechanism hypothesis; use matched clean/corrupt examples, interventions, counterexamples and a rescue test; report where the explanation fails | Intact baseline, matched random/resampling control, alternative pathways and distribution-shift checks; distinguish correlation, causal contribution and unique mechanism |
| AI for Science: R05, B01–B10, S01–S12 | One domain question with a simple baseline, measurement/label provenance, entity/homology/time controls, valid uncertainty and a falsifiable domain claim | Split and candidate-set sensitivity, physical/biological plausibility, orthogonal evidence and qualified review; computation alone does not establish experimental truth |

These criteria are operational inferences from scientific methodology and the retained curriculum. They are not a uniform industry hiring rubric or an empirically validated frontier-competence score. Whole-competency claims remain open in the accompanying 109-row matrix.
