# Evidence base and important limitations

**Learning technique evidence (general learners / STEM):**
- Dunlosky et al. (2013), practice testing and distributed practice high-utility: https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100612453266
- Karpicke & Roediger (2008), retrieval improves delayed recall in their experimental paradigm: https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1152408
- Freeman et al. (2014), meta-analysis of active learning in undergraduate STEM: https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1319030111

**Scientific reproducibility:**
- REFORMS reporting standards (2023): https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.07832
- NeurIPS official paper checklist: https://neurips.cc/public/guides/PaperChecklist

**Cautions:**
- These studies support specific learning mechanisms, **not** a guarantee that a 40 h/week self-directed curriculum creates research genius.
- Zulearn 'equivalent' contest formats have not been calibrated statistically against official IMO, Putnam, ICPC or lab qualifiers.
- Independent research is more than coursework; expert collaborators and external criticism are essential to calibrate quality.
