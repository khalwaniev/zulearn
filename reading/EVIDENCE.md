# Evidence register and limitations

## Learning science
- Dunlosky et al. (2013): practice testing and distributed practice rated high utility; findings are not a direct trial of AI scientist education. https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100612453266
- Karpicke & Roediger (2008): retrieval practice supports long-term retention in experimental learning. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1152408
- Trumble et al. (2024): systematic review distributed/retrieval in health professions; 43 of 63 experiments showed significant benefits. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37615780/
- Freeman et al. (2014): active learning meta-analysis in undergraduate STEM. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1319030111

## Research engineering caveats
- METR early-2025 randomized developer study: negative effect in their studied setting, not a universal causal estimate for every task or modern model. https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09089
- 2026 METR update: new study subject to serious selection bias and uncertain productivity impact. https://metr.org/blog/2026-02-24-uplift-update/

## Claim taxonomy
Direct evidence / extrapolation / engineering hypothesis / personal target. Keep these apart. There is no study proving a multi-year learning plan makes someone equal to particular Nobel laureates or leading scientists.
