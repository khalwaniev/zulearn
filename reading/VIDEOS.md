## Your own video output
One monthly 8–15 min technical defense video (optional if it steals from primary research): (1) thesis, (2) derivation, (3) experiment, (4) limitations, (5) unresolved question. Never claim an external course credential or technical result you have not earned. Respect datasets, licences, coauthors, and ethical review.
