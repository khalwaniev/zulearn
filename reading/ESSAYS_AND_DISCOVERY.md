# Quanta, Aeon, JSTOR Daily and science news: source hierarchy

**Primary source priority:** original paper / data / code / textbooks > scientific reviews > expert lectures > journalism / essays > social posts.

- **Quanta Magazine** — https://www.quantamagazine.org/ : priority 1 among magazines. Math, basic life sciences, theoretical CS; use 1–2 pieces weekly *only if they point to a significant research question*.
- **Aeon** — https://aeon.co/ : optional once per month for philosophy of science, epistemology, ethics and broad thinking. Not a formal technical course.
- **JSTOR Daily** — https://daily.jstor.org/ : occasional historical/social context and intellectual breadth. Not required reading for scientific-ML preparation.
- **Nature Methods, Nature Biotechnology, Bioinformatics, PLOS Computational Biology, JMLR, TMLR, ICLR, ICML, NeurIPS, CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, MICCAI** — select research papers directly, not all issues.
- **arXiv/bioRxiv** — discovery sources; label non-peer-reviewed status and date.
- **PubMed / Europe PMC / Crossref / Semantic Scholar / Google Scholar** — literature navigation with author identity checks.

**Weekly maximum:** 30–60 min reading editorial summaries, then use their citations to obtain primary sources. Never try to exhaustively read any magazine.
