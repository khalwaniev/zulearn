# Research-Adjusted Olympiad, Putnam, Miklós Schweitzer, IOI and ICPC

## Principle
Preserve task forms, proof rigor, conceptual surprise, independence and time pressure. **Do not claim psychometric equivalence** with IMO/Putnam/Miklós/IOI/ICPC. The original contests assess different subjects and candidate populations. Source their official archives for calibration, not prestige substitution.

| Inspiration | Zulearn exam | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| IMO | 4.5-hour theorem/inequality/discrete math proof round | Original, complete proofs with boundary cases |
| Putnam | Two 3-hour mixed mathematics papers | Unexpected derivations / counterexamples |
| Miklós Schweitzer | 7–10-day take-home research mathematics set | Open-ended proofs with full citations, no AI; audit originality |
| IOI | 5-hour individual algorithmic programming | Fast algorithms, hidden correctness tests, memory/time caps |
| ICPC | 5-hour multi-problem sprint (solo simulation, labelled as such) | Algorithmic design / debugging under time pressure |
| Research laboratory | 1–4-week experimental challenge | Reproducible baseline, ablation, uncertainty, paper critique |

## Important fairness
An ICPC World Finals team consists of multiple contestants; a solo simulation is not an official ICPC result. The official competition tasks and medals cannot be earned retroactively. Paper difficulty and research impact are not captured by solving speed alone.

## Research-adjusted sample problems
1. **Leakage theorem (3 h, 25 pts):** Construct a hierarchical data-generating process with patient-specific latent variables for which image-level random CV yields asymptotically optimistic ROC-AUC relative to patient-level CV. Specify distributional assumptions; prove an inequality or construct a counterexample using explicit distributions; design a consistent validation estimator.
2. **SE(3) invariance (3 h, 25 pts):** Prove rigid-motion invariance of pairwise-distance features for coordinates in R^3. Construct a pair of non-congruent labeled molecular point clouds indistinguishable to a chosen distance-only model (if assumptions permit); discuss chirality and oriented features.
3. **GO-DAG inference (5 h, 25 pts):** Given directed child->parent GO edges and noisy per-protein probabilities, compute ontology-consistent scores minimizing squared deviation subject to ancestor monotonicity, with a correctness argument; contrast exact and approximate strategies.
4. **Spectral matching (5 h, 25 pts):** Develop an efficient peak-matching dynamic program under tolerances and monotone one-to-one matching; analyze complexity and numerical tolerance issues. Build adversarial tests.
5. **Scientific qualifier (10 days, 100 pts):** Reproduce a small published biomedical-image or sequence modeling baseline using frozen data splits, generate uncertainty intervals, falsify at least one tempting but unsupported claim, and write a transparent methods report.

These are prompts, NOT solved problems or verified calibrated contest-difficulty items.
